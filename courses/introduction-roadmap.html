Prepare for this course

STARTING POINT · ZERO PREREQUISITES

Your first analysis, step by step

Who it is for: First-time learners. No spreadsheet, SQL or coding skills needed. Bring: A notebook, calculator or optional spreadsheet.

Your outcome: Define a specific question; inspect a small dataset; calculate revenue, costs and profit correctly; explain a cautious recommendation and its limitations.

Time: The eight lesson headings give their own approximate reading and practice times; your pace may vary. No fixed schedule or automatic pass is implied.

Final artifact: A one-page five-day kiosk analysis with a question, calculations, quality checks, recommendation and limitation. Use the fictional kiosk CSV and the independently calculated daily controls, which is separate from the six-order specialist training data.

Self-assessment: Tick a lesson only after you can reproduce its key example, then take the 12-question knowledge check. Self-reported completion is not instructor review.
