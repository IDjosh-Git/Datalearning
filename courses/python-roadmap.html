Module-by-module study plan

Open any module to return directly to its lesson.

  1. MODULE 01Python setup, files and analyst mindsetRun a script that reads the CSV, prints (6,12) and checks Completed=5, Returned=1.
  2. MODULE 02Variables, types and control flowCompute 300,000 using a loop or expression and explain why the returned order is excluded.
  3. MODULE 03Functions, collections and reusable scriptsCreate a function that rejects negative inputs and passes tests for two known orders.
  4. MODULE 04pandas selection and filteringUse pandas filtering and .copy(); assert there are 5 completed orders and 578,000 revenue.
  5. MODULE 05Cleaning, nulls, typing and joinsAssert unique order IDs and document a deliberate bad-row test and safe handling.
  6. MODULE 06groupby, pivot and business KPI reportingGroup by region and match East 316,000, West 190,000, North 72,000.
  7. MODULE 07Statistics and uncertaintyCalculate mean 115,600 and median 72,000, then explain what they cannot establish.
  8. MODULE 08NumPy vectors and matrix intuitionVerify vector multiplication and shape; explain when broadcasting can cause mistakes.
  9. MODULE 09Time series, charts and testable forecastsCreate a correctly ordered monthly series and describe why 2 months cannot justify forecasting.
  10. MODULE 10Python analytics capstoneDeliver reproducible code/notebook, dependency notes, tests, a summary table and narrative.
