Prepare for this course

COURSE BRIEF · Excel for Data Analysis

Learn it, verify it, use it

Who it is for: New analysts who want to clean records, audit calculations and deliver a usable workbook.

Before you begin: Introduction to Data Analysis (recommended); ability to open and save files. No coding required.

Setup and limitations: Excel for Microsoft 365, Excel 2024 or Excel 2021 recommended. XLOOKUP is unavailable in Excel 2016/2019; use INDEX/MATCH as provided. Desktop Excel is preferable for full PivotTable and dashboard work. The offline Arena evaluator supports only a subset of formulas.

Suggested commitment: Suggested study plan: 2–4 hours each week for 10 modules; actual time depends on prior Excel experience. This is a study plan, not timed platform tracking.

By the end: Build an auditable multi-sheet workbook: validated source table, repeatable KPI formulas, PivotTables, chart, dashboard and a short decision note.
