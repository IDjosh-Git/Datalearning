Administration and privacy

Accounts are created through Supabase Authentication. Every learner has a unique record in learner_profiles. Lesson records, quiz results and assessments are linked to that user ID. Learners cannot read another learner's rows. Administrator privileges can only be granted in the secure Supabase backend, never by self-registration.
