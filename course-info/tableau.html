What learners will be able to do

From navigating Tableau and data connections through calculations, charts, dashboards, stories and portfolio delivery.

  • Become familiar with Tableau's start page, data source pane, shelves, Marks card and sheet/dashboard/story tabs.
  • Set data types and link tables while respecting their analytical grain.
  • Choose the right shelf/marks combinations for rankings and trends.

Is this course right for you?

Review the weekly learning outcomes to assess your existing skills. Formal prerequisites have not been confirmed.

Tools: Tableau environment; confirm available edition and platform. Software licensing and platform support must be checked before enrolling. Where supported, learners may use a compatible spreadsheet application, but features can differ. Power BI Desktop requires Windows; browser-based Power BI is not a full replacement for Desktop authoring.

Course at a glance

10 weekly modules, 40 curriculum self-check questions. This count does not include separate mastery assessments.

How learning works

Learn the concept, review a worked example, complete the hands-on practice, check understanding, and reflect on mistakes. Open solutions only after attempting the task.

Hint: where to start

Averaging row-wise margins and reporting that as overall portfolio margin without justification.

Worked example and explanation (open after trying)
Business example: Finance stakeholders need consistent margin KPIs across filters and charts. Worked solution or syntax:
[Profit] = [Sales] - [Cost]
[Margin %] = SUM([Profit]) / SUM([Sales])

For revenue 1000 and cost 700, profit is 300 and margin 30%; aggregate ratios should be computed from total components.

FAQs

Are weekly exercises graded?

The syllabus identifies guided practice and self-check questions. Formal grading, retries and pass thresholds have not been verified; instructor capstone decisions are separate from weekly completion.

What if I cannot install the required software?

Review the listed tools before starting. Equivalent tools may not support the same features; contact the course owner for an approved alternative rather than assuming compatibility.

Can I browse without an account?

Yes. Course outlines are public; interactive lessons and saved progress require sign-in.

Does completing a lesson earn a certificate?

No certificate is promised by lesson completion. Eligibility and instructor-assessed requirements must be confirmed.
