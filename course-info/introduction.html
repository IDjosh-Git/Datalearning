What learners will be able to do

Start with what data and data analysis mean, learn the analytical process, then build trustworthy KPIs, visuals and business recommendations.

  • Define data and data analysis, distinguish raw records from insights, explain the analyst role and four types of analytics, and complete a first guided sales investigation.
  • Become confident reading CSVs and spreadsheets and recognising the meaning of each field.
  • Understand what one row represents and why duplicates can be legitimate.

Week 1: Introduction to Data Analysis and the Analyst Workspace

Learning outcome: Define data and data analysis, distinguish raw records from insights, explain the analyst role and four types of analytics, and complete a first guided sales investigation.

Estimated time: Not verified.

Hands-on task · practice: Explain data and data analysis in your own words. Calculate both product revenues and total revenue, identify the larger revenue contributor, and write one business question that requires additional data.

Activity type: Practice (completion requirement not verified); knowledge check is a self-check. Assessment status is not confirmed.

Success criteria (practice guidance, not a grading rubric): Produce the requested output, explain how it was obtained, check it against the example and identify one possible error or limitation.

Hint: where to start

Confusing data with insight, revenue with profit, or treating two example products as enough evidence to predict future demand.

Worked example and explanation (open after trying)
A Lagos kiosk sells 2 notebooks at ₦1,500 each and 3 pens at ₦500 each. Revenue = (2 × 1,500) + (3 × 500) = ₦4,500. Notebooks generate ₦3,000 and pens ₦1,500. The notebook category contributes more revenue, but revenue alone does not establish profit.

Notebook revenue ₦3,000; pen revenue ₦1,500; total ₦4,500. Distinguish revenue from profit and explain that more transactions or costs would be needed to recommend inventory changes.

Checkpoint · knowledge check (self-check): 4 existing questions. Sign in to study and answer Week 1 questions.

FAQs

Are weekly exercises graded?

The syllabus identifies guided practice and self-check questions. Formal grading, retries and pass thresholds have not been verified; instructor capstone decisions are separate from weekly completion.

What if I cannot install the required software?

Review the listed tools before starting. Equivalent tools may not support the same features; contact the course owner for an approved alternative rather than assuming compatibility.

Yes. Course outlines are public; interactive lessons and saved progress require sign-in.

Does completing a lesson earn a certificate?

No certificate is promised by lesson completion. Eligibility and instructor-assessed requirements must be confirmed.
