What learners will be able to do

Build trustworthy AI-assisted analytics in Excel, SQL, Power BI, Python and business reporting—with independent verification at every step.

  • Explain what AI can and cannot establish, protect data, and distinguish generated suggestions from verified evidence.
  • Write reusable analytical prompts with business objective, schema, scope, constraints and output format.
  • Use AI to propose formulas and cleaning steps, then validate them in Excel.

Is this course right for you?

Review the weekly learning outcomes to assess your existing skills. Formal prerequisites have not been confirmed.

Tools: AI assistant and a data analysis tool; verify access and data-sharing rules. Software licensing and platform support must be checked before enrolling. Where supported, learners may use a compatible spreadsheet application, but features can differ. Power BI Desktop requires Windows; browser-based Power BI is not a full replacement for Desktop authoring.

Course at a glance

10 weekly modules, 50 curriculum self-check questions. This count does not include separate mastery assessments.

How learning works

Learn the concept, review a worked example, complete the hands-on practice, check understanding, and reflect on mistakes. Open solutions only after attempting the task.

Assessments and progress

Weekly knowledge checks are self-checks, not instructor grades. Sign in to access interactive quizzes and progress saving. The capstone workflow checks that ten weekly completions are cloud-saved before submission; offline or locally marked completion alone does not satisfy that requirement. Quiz retries, scoring rules and other synchronization conditions must be confirmed against the running application. Lesson completion is not the same as instructor-assessed capstone approval.

Capstone project

The learning pathway includes a capstone workflow. The existing submission workflow requires all ten weeks to be completed and cloud-saved before submission; an instructor review status is tracked. Course-specific deliverables, evidence format, rubric, reviewer identity and pass mark require owner confirmation. Sign in to open the capstone workspace.

FAQs

Are weekly exercises graded?

The syllabus identifies guided practice and self-check questions. Formal grading, retries and pass thresholds have not been verified; instructor capstone decisions are separate from weekly completion.

What if I cannot install the required software?

Review the listed tools before starting. Equivalent tools may not support the same features; contact the course owner for an approved alternative rather than assuming compatibility.

Can I browse without an account?

Yes. Course outlines are public; interactive lessons and saved progress require sign-in.

Does completing a lesson earn a certificate?

No certificate is promised by lesson completion. Eligibility and instructor-assessed requirements must be confirmed.
