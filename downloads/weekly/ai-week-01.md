## Check your result (after attempting)
Expected evidence: A good response identifies likely data types but flags uncertainty about field definitions. To validate it, compare a small hand-checked sample with the final output, test an alternative filter or a changed input, and record any unmatched, missing or suspicious data. If results differ, review the original data, formula or query, aggregation grain and selected filters before sharing the report. Avoid this common error: Believing a confident response is automatically accurate or uploading private business spreadsheets.
