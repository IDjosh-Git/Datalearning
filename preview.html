FOUNDATION · 12 MIN

What is data analysis?

Data is recorded information: dates, prices, quantities, survey answers, journey times or electricity readings. Data analysis is the process of examining that information to answer a question, explain what happened and support a decision. The key is to find meaning, not merely to produce a spreadsheet or colourful chart.

One-minute example

You spent ₦2,000, ₦3,000 and ₦1,000 on transport over three days. The records are data. Adding them gives ₦6,000, a summary. Noticing that Tuesday was the most expensive day is an insight. Checking whether a cheaper route works on Tuesdays is a possible action.

Information, insight and decisions are different

RecordWhat happened?

Tuesday transport cost ₦3,000.

ExplainWhat does it mean?

It cost more than the other two days.

Act and measureWhat next?

Try another route; record time and cost.

Analysis can describe the past, explore why something happened, estimate a possible future or compare options. A forecast is uncertain; it is not a promise. You do not need to master statistics or coding before you can begin asking useful questions.

Try it (2 minutes)

Write down one number you record in daily life, a question it could answer, and one decision you might make after checking the answer.
