## Usage
Open index.html or a course HTML file; use the studio cards, enter responses, reveal model answers and mark topics mastered. Progress is saved in that browser profile, not synced across devices.

## Editorial note
The practice activities are self-assessed and not automatically evaluated. Formula compatibility and SQL dialects differ by software edition. Check examples with your target software. The offline tutor is reference-based, not a generative LLM.
