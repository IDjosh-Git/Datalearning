Course completion guide

Work through each week and complete the practice and quizzes. Your progress is saved to your account so you can continue on another device. Once all ten weeks are complete and your progress is up to date, submit your final project with supporting files and screenshots. Your instructor will review your work and share a score and feedback. A score of 70/100 or higher qualifies you for a course-completion certificate, which you can view in the Capstone Studio.

These certificates document your completed training and assessed achievement. If your connection is interrupted, your progress stays on this device until it can be saved to your account.
