Course completion guide

Work through each week and complete the practice and quizzes. Your progress is saved to your account so you can continue on another device. Once all ten weeks are complete and your progress is up to date, submit your final project with supporting files and screenshots. Your capstone is assessed using the shared rubric by AI when supported, or by an instructor when manual review is required. Certificates appear here after an eligible result is approved.

These certificates document your completed training and assessed achievement. If your connection is interrupted, check the cloud sync status and retry saving your progress when reconnected.
