How your progress and completion record work

Sign in, finish weekly lessons and quizzes, and check that Cloud Sync says complete. After all ten weeks are synced, submit a capstone project with files and screenshots in the Capstone Studio. Your instructor reviews your files and publishes the score and feedback once marking is finished. A passing score of at least 70/100 qualifies for a registered completion certificate. Open the Capstone Studio to view your score, feedback and printable certificate.

These are self-paced course-completion records, not accredited professional certifications. If syncing fails, your progress stays on this device until you retry.
