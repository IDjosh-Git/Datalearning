Build a project that speaks for itself.

Required for certification: Every 10-week specialist course concludes with an independent capstone reviewed by an instructor. Start planning in Week 1 and submit once all ten weeks are complete and synced. The passing assessment score is 70/100.

What you are building

Choose a real business question, use a lawful dataset, clean and validate it, perform a reproducible analysis, explain your findings and propose practical recommendations. Your project must show your own understanding, not just a finished chart.

10-week project roadmap

  1. Week 1: Understand requirements and select a problem.
  2. Weeks 2–3: Identify dataset, define questions, success measures and scope.
  3. Weeks 4–5: Prepare data; document missing values, duplicates, types and quality checks.
  4. Weeks 6–7: Analyse and verify key results.
  5. Week 8: Create a report, dashboard or notebook.
  6. Week 9: Document findings, limitations, recommendations and supporting evidence.
  7. Week 10: Final quality review and submission through Capstone Studio.

Required project structure

  1. Title and business problem: Who needs the analysis, and what decision does it support?
  2. Dataset and source: Describe the data, permissions, fields, time range and limitations.
  3. Preparation: Explain cleaning, transformations and checks.
  4. Analysis: Define KPIs, calculations and the approach used.
  5. Results: Provide readable tables, screenshots, visuals or query outputs.
  6. Recommendations: State 2–3 evidence-backed actions and any caveats.
  7. Reproducibility: Attach your original analysis file or script where possible.

How to submit

How to submit

  1. Complete all ten weeks and ensure your progress is synced.
  2. Open Capstone Studio and select the matching course.
  3. Enter your full name, project title and a project summary of at least 80 characters.
  4. Attach 1–4 supporting files, each no more than 12 MB and 32 MB combined. Accepted formats include screenshots, PDF, XLSX, SQL, CSV, PBIX, PY and IPYNB.
  5. Submit for instructor review and monitor the assessment status.

Do not upload confidential client, employer or personal data without permission. Avoid presenting unverified AI-generated work as your own analysis.
